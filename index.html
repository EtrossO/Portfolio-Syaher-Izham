Selected work

Campus Shield

Final year project, UPTM

An AI-powered phishing scanner for URLs and QR codes, built as a fully client-side single-page app with no server-side backend. Detection runs through four layers: rule-based heuristics (IP checks, brand spoofing, typosquatting, IDN homograph attacks, shorteners, phishing keywords, open-redirect detection), an on-device TensorFlow.js neural network trained on 33 URL features, the Google Safe Browsing API, and semantic analysis via Gemini 2.0 Flash. It also handles live camera QR scanning, local scan history, and a threat heatmap of aggregated domain data.
